We were pleased to present our recent work at **EMNLP 2026** and its co-located events in **Budapest, Hungary**. Across the main conference, Findings, ArabicNLP, and workshops, our contributions explored multilingual and multimodal reasoning, speech language models, culturally grounded evaluation, content safety, and resilience against misinformation.

## EMNLP Main and Findings

- **[MemeLens: Multilingual Multitask VLMs for Memes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12539)** introduces a unified multilingual vision-language model for understanding memes across multiple tasks, including propaganda and hate detection.
- **[MENASpeechBank: A Reference Voice Bank with Persona-Conditioned Multi-Turn Conversations for SpeechLLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07036)** provides a large speech and conversation resource covering English, Modern Standard Arabic, and regional Arabic varieties.
- **[Multi-turn Conversational AI from Text to Multimodal Interaction](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.2467/)** surveys data, models, and evaluation for conversational systems spanning text, speech, vision, video, and tool use.

## ArabicNLP 2026

- **[AHA-Memes: A Fine-Grained Multimodal Benchmark for Hate Detection in Arabic Memes](https://aclanthology.org/2026.arabicnlp-main.27/)** presents a benchmark for identifying hatefulness, attack types, and targets in Arabic memes.
- **[ImageEval 2026: Culturally Grounded Arabic Multimodal Evaluation](https://aclanthology.org/2026.arabicnlp-sharedtasks.84/)** evaluates spoken visual question answering, hallucination detection, and culturally accurate image generation in Arabic and English.
- **[ArGuard Shared Task: Harmful Content Detection in Arabic Memes and LLM Prompts](https://aclanthology.org/2026.arabicnlp-sharedtasks.39/)** introduces shared evaluation tracks for harmful Arabic memes and unsafe prompts directed at LLMs.

## Co-located Workshops

We also presented **CritiSense** at the Workshop on NLP for Positive Impact, **MENASpeechBank** at SALMA, **Building Multimodal QA for Everyday Knowledge** at ORACLE, and our multi-turn conversational AI survey at IMPACT-SPEECH. These sessions provided valuable opportunities to discuss practical, culturally inclusive, and responsible multilingual AI with the broader research community.
