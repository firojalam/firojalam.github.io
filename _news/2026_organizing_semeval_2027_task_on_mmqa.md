We are happy to share that we will be organizing a **SemEval 2027** shared task on **Multilingual and Multimodal Culturally Grounded Question Answering**.

The task focuses on culturally grounded visual question answering, where a system is given an image and a question and must generate a short, open-ended answer. The question may be presented as **speech or text**, in multiple languages including **Arabic (MSA and dialectal varieties), Bangla, English, Hindi, Assamese, Nepali, Urdu, Italian**.

An example from the **OASIS** dataset is shown below:

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/publication_preview/oasis_sample.png" title="MMCultureQA example from OASIS" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" width="500px" %}

The shared task includes two tracks:

- **Task 1:** Spoken Visual QA
- **Task 2:** Textual Visual QA

More information is available on the official task website: [https://mmcultureqa-semeval27.github.io/](https://mmcultureqa-semeval27.github.io/)
